# Why v1 pre-labels differ from Jakob's labels (rb_mixed 13-49)

Written by Claude. Method: for every class in `BRIEF.md`, gripper traces (`observation.state` gripper.pos) and top/wrist frames around the changed timestamps in `corrections.jsonl`, plus stripe-pixel counts (HSV mask of `scripts/recolour/target_mask.py`, 5 Hz) from both cameras.

1. **No visibility changes over time.** v1 sets only the start. Jakob's `s_partly` comes 0.4-2.7 s (mostly 1.0-1.7 s) after the hole release of the first clear that uncovers the stripe. Cue: top-camera stripe pixels (bowl crop) go from about 0 to 40 or more and stay for two samples after a `*_hole`. Rule: `s_partly` at max(first such sample, hole + 1.0 s). Examples: ep 4 (hole 8.81, human 9.93), ep 14 (14.09, 15.28), ep 29 (12.27, 13.44). Misses: ep 7, 22 and 39, where the stripe flickers in and out under the arm.
2. **Start state too visible.** At t=0.2-0.8 s the stripe pixel median is 0 for 24 of 27 buried starts, and 40-900 for partly and visible. Cue: v1 says partly or visible but the median is under 30 px: it is buried (eps 14, 15, 32, 39, 40, 45, 47). Partly vs visible cannot be told apart by pixels (visible 178-904, partly 40-817); that stays a judgement call.
3. **`wrist_blind` never added.** Jakob adds it from about ep 20 on (none before), 20 of 37 episodes. Cue: the smallest wrist-camera stripe pixel count in the 2 s before the final `s_grab` is under about 3000, i.e. the ball is mostly out of the wrist view or covered; time about 0.9 s before the grab. Fitted on eps 20-34, tested on 35-49: 8 of 11 found, 1 false alarm.
4. **Wrong ball on a miss.** v1 assumes striped. Cue: the miss comes right before a red or blue target or grab (within 2.5 s) while the stripe is not yet uncovered: the miss and its target belong to that ball. Fixes eps 16, 18, 22, 45 and 48's first miss; ball-event precision goes 0.936 to 0.957, exact episodes 24 to 26.
5. **Missing quick attempts.** Not rule-able from the joints alone: of 15 gripper closes (jaws open before, closes below 17.5, no v1 label, not the return from a hole) only 6 have a Jakob label nearby. He leaves most failed pinches on covering balls unlabelled (eps 25, 28, 32, 38, 41, 44, 48 all have such a close with no label), so v2 lists them in `uncertain` and does not add events. Where he does label them (35, 38, 39, 40, 41) the ball colour and slip-vs-miss need the wrist frame at the close.
6. **Retimed grabs.** Eps 6 and 40: the label sits while the jaws are still open (gripper reading above 20) and the real close follows 0.4-0.9 s later. Rule: a `*_grab` placed where the gripper reads above 20 moves to the next close plus 0.15 s. Does not change the numbers on the 37 episodes (nothing else matched).
