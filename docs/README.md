# docs/

Runbooks for the people at the robot: which command to run and what to check. Why things are the way they are is in `decisions/`; verified facts about our setup are in `kb/`.

- [setup.md](setup.md): one-time setup of a laptop and of the robot laptop (WSL, USB, environments, calibration, gripper torque cap).
- [demo.md](demo.md): running the demo: preflight, the three terminals, starting rounds, stopping, troubleshooting, logs.
- [voice.md](voice.md): what you can say, adding phrases, and the standalone voice program.
- [dance.md](dance.md): the replayed dance.
- [finger-biting.md](finger-biting.md): the experimental finger-bite round.
- [recording.md](recording.md): recording teleoperated episodes from the dashboard or a terminal, checking and uploading them.
- [curation-dashboard.md](curation-dashboard.md): browsing, labelling and mixing episodes for training.
- [training.md](training.md): training ACT on Modal or locally, fine-tuning on new data, run-time settings.
- [evaluation.md](evaluation.md): comparing pick policies on the arm with `eval_pick.sh`.
- [camera-calibration.md](camera-calibration.md): calibrating the top camera and cross-checking it.
- [hand-tracking.md](hand-tracking.md): checking the hand tracker and the arm kinematics, and hand following on its own.
- [HISTORY.md](HISTORY.md): timeline of the hackathon: ideas, models and datasets tried, results.
