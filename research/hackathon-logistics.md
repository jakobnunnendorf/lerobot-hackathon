# Physical AI Hackathon Bilbao (Hackathon LeRobot Bilbao, 2nd edition) - logistics research

Researched 2026-11-20 from the official site, the registration platform, archived first-edition pages and third-party coverage. Every fact has its source URL. "Not found" means the source does not state it. Anything labelled **inferred** is my reading, not a published fact.

## TL;DR

1. **Fri 20 Nov 16:00 to Sun 22 Nov 13:30 (2026), La Perrera Espazioa, Sabino Arana 50, Bilbao.** Hacking starts Fri 18:00. **Hard deadline Sun 11:00: "presentation, dataset and code"**. Presentations Sun 11:30-12:30, **5 minutes per team**, live demo in front of a jury. Awards 13:00-13:30.
2. **Prizes: 1st 1,500 EUR, 2nd 900 EUR, 3rd 600 EUR** (3,000 EUR total). No tracks, themes or special categories are published for this edition.
3. **Judging criteria and weights for 2026: not published.** The 2025 edition's FAQ listed four unweighted criteria: innovation, technical feasibility, potential impact, quality of the presentation. Jury members: not published.
4. **Provided: one SO-101 per team (assembled and calibrated), a workstation with cameras, mentors all weekend. Bring only a laptop.** GPUs, cloud credits, camera count/placement, LeRobot version: not stated.
5. **Rules, safety rules, pre-built-work policy and team size limit are not published for 2026** (2025 edition: teams of 2-5). The 2025 winner was a voice-driven "AI bartender" (ACT policy, 80 episodes) demoed live at the team's own table.

## 5. Previous editions and similar LeRobot hackathons

**Bilbao first edition (21-23 Nov 2025, La Perrera)**

- Scale: "+120 participantes, +20 equipos, +20 proyectos, +5 mentores". Source: https://hackathonbilbao.eus/home-page-es
- Same agenda as 2026, including "Deadline Submissions: Presentacion + Dataset + Codigo" at 11:00 and "Presentaciones 5 min por equipo" 11:30-12:30. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20251106011720/https://hackathonbilbao.eus/
- The city press release gave a different final-day timing for 2025: presentations 09:30-12:30, prizes 12:30-13:30. Source: https://bilbaogazte.bilbao.eus/es/2025/11/19/bilbao-se-suma-a-la-semana-europea-de-la-robotica/
- Pre-event talk by Asier Arranz "de la mano de NVIDIA" at EHU on 23 Oct 2025 as an introduction to the hackathon. Source: https://www.ehu.eus/es/web/zientzia-teknologia-fakultatea/-/hitzaldia-asier-arranz

**2025 winner: "AI Bartender", team Megas XLR (1st place, per the team's own account)**

Source for all points: https://elchinaslanli.com/projects/how-we-won-the-lerobot-hackathon-2025-meet-the-ai-bartender and https://www.udg.edu/en/masters-en-tecnologia/mirs/petjada-mirs/aslanli

- Five people who formed the team at the event.
- Pipeline: speech-to-text, then an LLM (Gemini) giving the bartender a personality, then the robot pours the ordered drink. Drinks were coloured sand: blue for "water", red for "Coke".
- Policy: ACT, chosen for local inference without network latency. 50 episodes of pouring water plus 30 of pouring Coke. 20,000 steps, batch size 4. Trained overnight on NVIDIA Brev cloud GPUs, ready about 05:00 on Sunday.
- A tablecloth gave a uniform background.
- Problems: the robot stopped working on Saturday morning (they suspected the required Phosphobot platform) and they reinstalled Ubuntu; an attempt to add a third object was laggy and dropped; electronics issues appeared just before the demo.
- Demo: the jury came to the table and a crowd blocked the team from swapping cups, so the robot poured both drinks into one cup. The team presented it as a cocktail feature and the judges liked it.
- The author's own explanation of the win: top hackathon projects do "real", relatable tasks; the conversational LLM layer and multi-stage pipeline made it stand out. This is the participant's opinion, not jury feedback.
- Names of the 2nd and 3rd place projects: **not found**.

**Another 2025 Bilbao project**

- A team of six from PUCRA (UPC-linked association) built an autonomous LeKiwi system to find, pick and store green test tubes. Placing not stated. Known only from a search-result snippet of a LinkedIn profile that I could not open (HTTP 999), so treat as unverified. Source: https://www.linkedin.com/in/elena-villalba/

**LeRobot Worldwide Hackathon (14-15 June 2025, Hugging Face)**

- Over 3,000 participants from 44 countries, 250+ submissions; 30 winning teams. Source: https://x.com/LeRobotHF/status/1935302024540696637 (via search snippet)
- Prizes: 15,000 EUR of hardware with Seeed Studio. 1st-3rd: Hope Jr arm plus LeKiwi; 4th-5th: Hope Jr arm; 6th-24th: LeKiwi; 25th-30th: SO-101. Winners were picked from demo videos ("so many amazing demo videos"). Source: https://huggingface.co/spaces/LeRobot-worldwide-hackathon/all-winners
- Top ranks by video filename: 1 LeChef (team 22), 2 ez_2_ai (team 250), 3 ML6 (team 297), 4 Electrify (team 141), 5 data-surgeon (team 289). What each project did is not described on the page. Source: https://huggingface.co/api/datasets/maringetxway/all-winners/tree/main
- 2nd worldwide (ez_2_ai, two students): a robot that folds T-shirts and sorts pills into daily medicine boxes, motivated by helping a grandfather. Bimanual SO-100, four cameras, pi0 / SmolVLA / ACT, 50 episodes for folding, 70% success during the event. A sponsor praised that they "built, trained and deployed everything in just 48 hours". Source: https://theaiinnovator.com/team-behind-ai-robot-that-folds-laundry-wins-hackathon/
- Top-30 team (Munich): robot drawing and wiping with GR00T N1, deliberately not a pick-and-place demo. Lessons: camera position mattered most (small shifts caused failures); uniform backgrounds helped; SmolVLA teams did better on average with fewer episodes than GR00T teams; inverse kinematics was a time sink and imitation learning from day one would have gone further. Source: https://arturhabuda.com/2025/06/29/lerobot-worldwide-hackathon-how-we-got-into-the-top-30-worldwide/
- London chapter, "Most Novel Solution" (tied): SO-101 that picks up a sponge, wipes sauce off a plate and returns the sponge. Leader/follower pair, wrist camera plus overhead RealSense, 75 episodes, GR00T N1.5 and SmolVLA. Author's lessons: "optimize the demo, not just the codebase", a clear and testable problem, fixed team roles, a browser interface to visualise and control the robot. Source: https://darthgera123.github.io/blogs/hackathon/robot
- Official judging criteria for the Worldwide Hackathon: **not found**.

**Pattern across winners (inferred from the accounts above)**

- A task a bystander understands at once and that reads as useful or relatable (bartender, laundry and pills, wiping a plate).
- A learned policy working live on the real arm, with 50-80 episodes of one or two narrow skills, not a broad task set.
- An interaction layer on top of the policy (voice, LLM persona, web UI).
- Controlled scene: fixed cameras, uniform background.
- Training finished the night before, leaving margin for hardware failures on the last morning.
