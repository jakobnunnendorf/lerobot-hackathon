# How does the demo chain its skills, and who owns the robot while it does?

- Status: OPEN (2026-11-21, Jakob's agent, `scripts/supervisor/`). Built and tested with fakes and a simulated arm only; nothing has run on the arm, a camera or a real policy checkpoint.

## Decision

A thin state machine in one process that owns the robot connection (both cameras included) for the whole demo:

REST -> [LOOK, optional] -> PICK -> VERIFY_HOLD -> GIVE (hand) or PUT_DOWN (no hand, or voice says "container") -> REST -> DONE, with ABORT reachable from every state.

- **PICK is one run of `molnrb/act_pick_ball_combined`, also when the ball is buried.** That policy was trained on visible and buried episodes, and the buried ones dig and pick in one episode (molnrb's agent, board 7061614734). Balazs (board 7061650334): the combined policy grabs and digs end to end, "so no supervisor is needed for that part". So the supervisor does not split picking into look/dig steps.
- **REST is the folded rest pose, not a HOME above the bowl.** Every pick episode starts and ends there, holding the ball at the end (board 7061614734). In robot mode it is the pose at start, or `--rest`.
- **VERIFY_HOLD** takes the median gripper reading over 0.5 s with the arm still and logs it every time. 6-20 on the 0-100 scale (`--hold-range`) counts as holding. Measured by molnrb's agent over all 145 combined episodes (board 7061759444): holding min 9.3, 5th percentile 11.2, median 13.0, 95th percentile 15.1, max 16.4; empty about 0-2. Kamel's agent: open or half-open jaws read 17-40 (7061756343). A first band of 10-17 would have rejected the 9.3 hold. **Foil balls are not measured**: one may read inside the band. Not holding: back to REST and PICK again, at most `--max-pick-attempts` (3) picks, then the round ends as `gave_up`.
- **Grasp check inside PICK** (`scripts/supervisor/grasp_check.py`, on by default in robot mode, `--no-grasp-check`): VERIFY_HOLD only ran after PICK ended, so an empty close on the jaw tips was carried through the rest of the ACT chunk. The wrapper pauses at the commanded close (below 10 after above 20), holds the arm, sets the gripper goal to 1, and after 0.4 s judges held = reading in 6-20 and reading - goal >= 3. Evidence, 606 teleoperated closes in molnrb's four pick datasets, read 0.5 s after the close: closes carried to the end (ball held, 218) p1 10.9, median 12.9, 217 pass; all 78 reading below 4 (empty, 0.4-3.9) fail; 13 closes resting at 6-8 at their goal (error < 3) fail, which the band alone would call holding; readings settle within 0.5 s (p95 change 0.02/frame). Miss: open, back off 0.5 s along the commanded path, re-run PICK (fresh query) within the pick limit; held: re-query and watch for a slip during the lift. Present_Load of the gripper is read and logged (LeRobot 0.7.0 reads any STS3215 register through rustypot), not used until measured; the datasets hold positions only. An FK "too high" check exists but is off: tip z at holding closes 0.02-0.16 m, empty closes overlap.
- **Held-ball check (is it the target?):** the gripper band cannot tell the striped target (median 13.4) from a red or blue ball (12.7). After the band says holding, VERIFY_HOLD moves to REST (gripper held), takes 5 wrist frames and asks `Sensors.held_ball_area(frame, ball)` for the median area of each ball (all three logged as `held_areas_px`). The wanted ball (`striped`, or the asked colour) at or above its threshold: the target, carry on. Below it: `wrong ball`, never handed over: PUT_DOWN (the container of `Sensors.ball_colour` if that hook is set, else the generic put-down pose), then PICK again within `--max-pick-attempts`; out of picks the round ends as `wrong_ball`. In robot mode the hook is #24's `ColourVisibility` (`scripts/vision/supervisor_adapter.py`, branch `jakobnunnendorf/colour-pick`) found in `--vision-dir` (default `scripts/vision`; before #24 is merged point it at the colour-pick worktree's `scripts/vision`), with the top camera's bowl ROI replaced by the wrist region where the jaws hold the ball and no ball size limit.
  - **Region:** `--held-check-roi`, default ellipse `0.52 0.72 0.2 0.25`, the lower middle of the wrist frame at rest (PR #30, `kb/held-ball-wrist-areas.md`, board 7063126045: striped centre x 0.49-0.62, y 0.64-0.77 over 183 offline detections). The first guess `0.5 0.5 0.25 0.3` saw mostly ceiling; on whole frames blue read up to ~218,000 px whatever was held (board 7063041111).
  - **Thresholds:** striped `--min-held-target-area`, unset by default (the area is only logged, `held_check=unmeasured`); suggested start 120 (inside the region: 12.5% targets rejected, 4.5% covering balls accepted). Other balls `--min-held-area COLOUR=PX`; red's candidate 2000 is the default (at most 529 px with the target held, median ~15,000 with a red ball held), to confirm with one red ball held at rest.
  - **Unseen:** when every ball reads below `--held-unseen-floor` (30 px) the held ball is unseen, not wrong (a deep grasp holds it above the region; board 7065772934): it is kept as the gripper band says, logged `held_check: unseen`. With `--log`, each VERIFY_HOLD saves its wrist frame as `<log name>_verify_hold_r<round>_p<attempt>_<time>.jpg` next to the log (`wrist_frame` in the transition).
  - **Which balls are judged:** `--held-check-colours`, default `striped` only; red joins with `--held-check-colours striped red` once confirmed. Blue stays out and has no threshold: not usable on the wrist camera (up to 27,466 px inside the region with the target held; the bluish ceiling falls inside it). An unjudged ball logs `held_check=untrusted` and is accepted, the same as without the check.
  - **Misses:** the ~12.5% rejected targets are detector misses (#24's striped range tuned on the top camera, the wrist camera's cool white balance); re-tune it on wrist frames with #24's calibrate mode. A rejected target costs a re-pick, not safety.
  - Detector missing or `--no-held-check`: `held_check=off`, the band alone decides (the old behaviour, with a WARNING at start). Detector error or no wrist frame: `held_check=unavailable`, the ball is accepted as before and the error is logged; a broken camera should not make the arm put down every good ball.
- **Cameras:** both OpenCV cameras ask for MJPG (`--camera-fourcc`, as PR #6), because two uncompressed 640x480/30 fps streams usually do not fit on one USB bus; `none` turns it off.
- **Targets and containers (new table, board 7062034477):** the foil balls are gone; the bowl now has 11 red and 11 blue balls plus the striped target, and fixed red and blue sorting containers. Accepted targets: `striped`, `red`, `blue`. Destination `container` means the container of the ball's colour: two taught poses (`--container-pose-red`, `--container-pose-blue`), placeholders until taught at the table, falling back to `--put-down-pose` (which is also where the striped ball goes). The colour is `Sensors.ball_colour` (default: the colour asked for), a hook for the colour detector that another agent builds (`jakobnunnendorf/colour-pick`); `Skills.pick_colour` is the matching hook for picking a colour. Without it a colour round is refused before the arm moves (`unsupported_target`) rather than picking the wrong ball. CLEAR_GRAY is for the old foil setup only and stays off.
- **A command is for one round:** destination and target are cleared when a round ends (`Sensors.finish_round`), also after an abort or a refusal. Before this fix round 2 reused round 1's "hand" or "container" (dry run of #15+#13+#17+#21).
- **Shared memory:** the `FrameWriter` block is closed in a `finally` and a stale block of the same name is unlinked at start, so a crash or Ctrl-C cannot make the next start fail with FileExistsError.
- **Destination:** voice or keyboard "container" -> PUT_DOWN. Otherwise the ball is carried in joint space (gripper held) to the **present pose** (`--present-pose`, default `[0, -55.3, 5.6, 91.8, 0]`, tip about (0.15, 0, 0.20) m, board 7061460387) and the supervisor waits there up to `--hand-wait` (3 s) for the tracker to report two palms: then GIVE, else PUT_DOWN. Voice or key "hand" goes to GIVE without waiting. If the measured joints do not reach the present pose within 15 s: ABORT.
- **GIVE, two modes** (`--give-mode`): `follow` runs the #13 hand-following loop from the present pose; `present` (no camera calibration, no #13 loop needed, only the tracker's hand messages) holds the present pose over a taped spot and opens once two palms have been seen for 1 s. `auto` picks `follow` when #13's files are there. GIVE ends with `dropped`, `hand_lost` (the #13 loop's own 3 s, or 5 s without palms in `present` mode) or on timeout (45 s). Still holding after GIVE -> back to REST holding -> PUT_DOWN.
- **PUT_DOWN** (also "container"): carry the ball in joint space to a taught pose over the container (`--put-down-pose`, five joints read at the table) and open there; or replay a recorded episode (`--put-down-episode REPO:EP`) once one exists. Still holding afterwards -> ABORT.
- **LOOK** (`--look`) is a log-only pre-check: the median mask area of `scripts/recolour/target_mask.py` over 5 top-camera frames, threshold 150 px (`decisions/target-visibility-check.md`). It never stops a pick. With `--clear-gray-policy` it also routes "not visible" to CLEAR_GRAY (REST to REST), at most `--max-clear-attempts` (3) times, then PICK digs anyway. Off by default: no CLEAR GRAY dataset exists (board 7061614734).
- **SHAKE** has a hook (a state after GIVE, `Skills.shake`) but no skill. The team put the handshake after everything else (board 7061650334).
- **Hold value:** while holding, the gripper goal stays at the pick policy's last goal (the #13 rule: never loosen by guessing a number). `--gripper-hold` sets a fixed value after VERIFY_HOLD instead. A value inside the measured reading range (11-16) would ask for no more squeeze than the ball's width, so the ball could slip; that is why it is not the default.
- **Moves are closed loop:** a joint-space move is done only when the measured arm joints are within 4 degrees of the target, not when the last command was sent (review of #17 by Space0code's agent).
- **Speed cap:** `--max-step-deg` limits every arm joint command, from every skill including the policy, to that many degrees per tick. Off by default, because a capped policy no longer moves as it was trained.
- **Policy options:** `--n-action-steps` overrides the checkpoint's value (ACT default 100, 3.3 s per replan); `--camera-rename top=camera1 wrist=camera2` for SmolVLA checkpoints trained with Kamel's camera names (review of #17).
- **Limits:** a time budget per skill (PICK 40 s, CLEAR GRAY 30 s, GIVE 45 s, PUT DOWN 20 s, moves 15 s). A policy never says it is done, so PICK also ends early when the arm has been within 8 degrees of REST with the gripper in the holding range for 1 s (after at least 3 s; which joints count is below).
- **Safe states:** the gripper goal is never raised while the gripper reads holding, except inside GIVE (the #13 loop's drop) and PUT_DOWN. ABORT (key `a` + Enter, Ctrl-C, or any skill error) sends nothing more: the servos keep their last goal, torque stays on (`disable_torque_on_disconnect=False`), and the person at the table takes the arm.
- **PICK's early end only judges lift, elbow and wrist_flex, and only after the arm has left rest by more than 25° (Tian, 2026-11-21, `TianK003/demo-chain`)**. This is the rule of `scripts/eval/eval_pick.py`.
  - In the 80 rb_mixed episodes only those three joints end within 1° of the start pose; pan and roll vary. With all five joints required, a successful pick could miss the 8° window and run its full budget.
  - The training rest gripper (17.8) is inside the 6-20 holding band. Without the "left rest" rule, a policy that has not moved yet would end as "returned holding" after 4 s.
- **The grasp check locks the gripper after a hold and needs load ≥ 200 (2026-11-21, calibration run on `molnrb/arm-logs` 4e20743):** in r1 and r6 ACT re-opened on a held ball and closed again on nothing, which was judged a fresh miss and spent pick attempts; ball loads were 440-500, empty 28-44. `--no-grasp-lock-after-hold` restores the old flow.
- **The gripper torque is capped at 200, so the load threshold is 120 (2026-11-22):** squeezing at LeRobot's 500 stripped the gripper servo's gears; at 200 a ball is held firmly and its load reads the cap, 200. `--grasp-min-load` and `--hold-min-load` went from 200 to 120, between empty (28-44) and held (200). Facts and the do-not-exceed rule: `kb/gripper-torque-limit.md`.
- **A lost hand does not end GIVE (Tian, 2026-11-21).** The loop holds, goes back to the present pose after 5 s (`lost_to_home_s`) and follows the palm again when it reappears. GIVE ends on the drop or its 45 s budget, then REST → PUT_DOWN as before.
  - Rejected: ending GIVE as `hand_lost` the moment the loop goes home (the first version). In T2 on the arm the tracker printed `no hand` in 63 of 157 lines while the gripper hovered over the palm (board 7064755928), because the arm hides the palm from the side camera, so one occlusion would have cancelled the handover.
- **Speed cap in `demo_chain.sh`: `--max-step-deg 15`, eval_pick's `max_relative_target` (Tian, 2026-11-21).** It only stops a wild jump: from black test frames Tomi's 15K checkpoint asked for a first goal 50+ deg from rest.
  - Rejected: 3 deg/tick (Jakob's agent, 7065448559). In the 57,183 action frames of `molnrb/so101_pick_rb_mixed` the largest arm-joint step is above 3 deg in 17.3% of ticks, above 5 deg in 7.1%, and above 15 deg in 0.01% (wrist_flex p99 8.9 deg/tick). So 3 slows the policy on a sixth of its ticks, away from training and from the P runs (foil 15K 10/13, board 7065447840), which ran with 15. 1 deg/tick slows 54% of ticks.
  - GIVE keeps its own 1 deg/tick (`--give-max-step-deg`).
- **With the grasp check off, "holding" also needs |Present_Load| ≥ 200 (`--hold-min-load`; Jakob's go, board 7067344189).** In the VLA 5K take 2 run (`molnrb/arm-logs` 950a0c2, board 7067314601), PICK timed out with the gripper reading 15. The policy had commanded 14.25, the jaws had nothing squeezed, and the load read 32. The band alone called it a hold, and the arm carried an empty gripper to the present pose.
  - Every `holding()` caller uses the rule. With it in VERIFY_HOLD only, REST reads a hold again and loops back.
  - An unreadable load falls back to the band, as in the grasp check.
- **A camera stall of up to 2 s is waited out, not an ABORT (Jakob's go, 7067406967).** LeRobot 0.7.0's `read_latest` raises once the newest frame is over 500 ms old. The top camera reached 519 ms on the robot laptop's WSL USB, and that ABORT froze the arm mid-round.
  - While `RobotArm.observe` re-reads, nothing is sent, so the arm holds its last goal.
  - A camera silent for 2 s still aborts.
  - Each stall is printed and goes into the live status and the tick log.
- **An ABORT on an error logs `error`, `error_at` (file:line function), the gripper load, the last hold load and the pick count.** The failing state's own details are lost when it raises; take 2 lost its held-check verdict that way.
- **Log:** one JSONL line per transition and one `round_end` line per round with `outcome` (`given`, `put_down`, `gave_up`, `wrong_ball`, `aborted`, `unsupported_target`), `wrong_balls`, pick and clear attempts and the state list, for the H5/H7 counts.

## Robot ownership

- **One process, one connection, for the whole demo.** The supervisor connects the follower with both cameras once (ACT needs both) and never disconnects or switches torque off while running; every skill (policies, replays, the #13 loop) is called in that process and uses that connection. The hand tracker gets top-camera frames from the supervisor through `FrameWriter` every second tick in every state, between rounds included, as #13 does. Not reused: PR #6's `run_policy.sh`, which leaves the arm limp at the end.
- **Handing the arm to the #13 loop:** the supervisor calls `handover_loop.run()` in its own thread with an adapter for the arm (`run()` already takes any object with `read`, `send` and `top_frame`, so #13 needs no change and `connect_robot()` is not used), passing the present pose as the loop's HOME and the last commanded gripper value as its hold value. It stops the loop by raising from the loop's `on_tick` callback. The supervisor sends nothing while the loop runs, and the loop sends nothing after it returns. #13's files are imported, not changed.
- **Not a subprocess:** handing over to a second process means `disconnect()` here and `connect()` there, and `connect()` runs `configure()` with torque off on all six motors (`research/lerobot-multi-skill-runner.md`, section 1). That drops the ball held at that moment.
- **Tian's tested loop (2026-11-21, #13 at 47d437c or `TianK003/hand-tracking-test` 3bb344d):** GIVE passes the values of Balazs's arm run (hover 0.28 m, floor 0.20 m, half speed; board 7064755928) and `celebrate_cycles` (3), and ends `dropped` once the celebration is over and the gripper is open. It passes no `rest` to the loop: the fold is the supervisor's REST, a closed-loop joint move, so there is one rest move, not two. With Tian's offsets the present pose's tip is at 0.243 m (above the 0.20 m floor) and the whole round stays at 0.243-0.40 m in the model with Balazs's rest pose. Robot mode now also sets Goal_Position to the present position before `connect()` (Tian's fix in #13). Decision record of the chain: Space0code, board 7064763916.
- **The loop's HOME and REST are reconciled by the present pose.** The loop moves the tip in clamped Cartesian steps (0.12-0.38 m from the pan axis, 0.08-0.30 m high). Tried in the simulation (2026-11-21, `tests/supervisor_scenarios.py`) with the loop's HOME set to a folded rest pose (`[0, 20, 60, 90, 0]`, tip 3.4 cm behind the pan axis, 6.3 cm high): the arm never moved and the round ran into the 45 s timeout. With the joint-space move to the present pose first, the loop follows and drops, and the supervisor moves back to REST in joint space: given in 8.5 s.

## Alternatives

- **GIVE as a subprocess running `handover_loop.py --robot`** - rejected: drops the ball (above).
- **Copying the loop's tick body into the supervisor** - rejected: the safety rules would exist twice and drift. Raising from `on_tick` keeps the loop unchanged.
- **LOOK -> CLEAR GRAY -> LOOK as the default flow** (the first plan, `ideas/ball-picking-and-handover.md`) - replaced by one PICK run (above). Kept behind `--clear-gray-policy`.
- **Voice through our own stand-in listener on port 8765** - replaced by Tian's `CommandReceiver` from `scripts/voice/link.py` once #16 was merged; polled once per tick.
